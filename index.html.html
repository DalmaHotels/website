Welcome

Discover Serenity by the Sea

Nestled just 200 metres from the pristine ocean shores of Zanzibar, New Dalma Hotel offers a perfect blend of comfort, convenience, and natural beauty. Whether you're seeking a relaxing getaway, an adventurous escape, or a peaceful retreat, our hotel provides everything you need for an unforgettable stay.

Only 7 km from the bustling town centre, enjoy the best of both worlds — tranquility and accessibility. Explore nearby spice tours, historic Stone Town, or simply unwind on the white sandy beaches.
